Research

I started with robust and submodular optimization, including work on sensor selection and distributed methods. My PhD research then moved toward reinforcement learning and stochastic approximation. I study distributional and average-reward RL, decision-making with coupled dynamics, and learning from dependent data or noise with unbounded variance.

More recently, I’ve been working on LLM post-training. I’m interested in when low-rank evolution strategies behave differently from dense Gaussian methods, and whether they can make training less expensive. Our EGGROLL and LOO-ROLL work looks at those questions through both analysis and experiments on language models.
