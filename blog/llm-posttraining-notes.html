Notes on Learning LLM Post-Training

July 2026

This is a placeholder post for notes from my current effort to build practical fluency in modern language models and post-training. My background is in reinforcement learning, optimization, stochastic approximation, and submodular optimization, so the goal is not to pretend these topics are new to me in a week. The goal is to connect that background to the actual mechanics of decoder-only transformers, supervised fine-tuning, preference optimization, reward modeling, and evaluation.

Some of the topics I plan to write about here:

  • Tokenization and why tokens are not words.
  • Causal masking and the difference between training-time tensors and inference-time generation.
  • Greedy decoding, temperature, top-p sampling, and why decoding is not training.
  • LoRA supervised fine-tuning and what is actually being optimized.
  • Where reinforcement learning enters post-training, and where the analogy is weaker than it first looks.

I will replace this placeholder with a real post once the notes are more useful than merely autobiographical.
